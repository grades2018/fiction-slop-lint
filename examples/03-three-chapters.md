# Chapter 1

The harbor was quiet when Nora arrived. She stood at the end of the pier and watched the boats. She counted the masts. She checked her watch. She waited for Tom to come, but Tom did not come. She walked back along the pier. She bought a coffee at the kiosk. She sat down on the bench.

The old man at the kiosk looked at her. He looked at her for a long time. "You're the new teacher," he said. Nora nodded. She did not say anything else. She drank the coffee. She felt the cold in her hands.

# Chapter 2

The school was small and the corridors smelled of chalk. Nora walked down the corridor and opened the door to the classroom. She put her bag on the desk. She wrote her name on the board. She looked at the empty chairs. She wondered who would sit in them.

The children came in at eight. They sat down quietly. They looked at her. They looked at the board. A boy in the back row raised his hand. "Are you the new teacher?" he asked. Nora nodded. She did not say anything else. She felt the cold in her hands.

# Chapter 3

By March the harbor was busy again. Nora walked along the pier every morning and watched the boats. She knew the names of the fishermen now. She knew which of them drank and which of them prayed. She counted the masts. She checked her watch. She waited for the ferry, and the ferry came.

On the last day of term the boy in the back row brought her a shell. "For the new teacher," he said, though she was not new anymore. Nora nodded. She did not say anything else. She felt the warmth in her hands, and for once she did not mind the silence.
